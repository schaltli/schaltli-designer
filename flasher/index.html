Flash a ScreenBee device

A board fresh from the shop has no ScreenBee on it, and nothing to update over the air. This page writes the whole firmware onto it through the USB cable - no install, no drivers, no Python.

This browser cannot talk to a serial port. Chrome or Edge on a computer can; Firefox and Safari cannot, and neither can a phone.

A browser only offers serial ports on a secure page. Open this page over https.

No factory image has been published yet. Once a firmware release carries one, it appears here.
