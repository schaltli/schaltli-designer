# Cards

Capability map, agreed with the user on 2026-11-03 after looking at the
screens of CamperUI v1.9.1 (artifact «CamperUI im Vergleich»): almost every
value there sits in a card - a rounded, slightly lighter panel with an edge,
padding, a small heading and a large value - and the cards stand in a grid
with even gaps, some nested, some with an accent edge.

Schaltli can draw a card today (a free area with a fill, an edge and a
radius) but not conveniently: nothing in it follows when it grows, it has no
padding, a table cannot hold cards at even gaps, and blocks arrive as loose
objects.

**Decisions** (the user, 2026-11-03):

- **No built-in title.** A card does not draw a heading of its own: a
  heading is whatever the user puts at its top - a text, an icon and a text,
  anything. Composition, not a card type that knows about titles.
- **Anchor as the model, plain words in the UI.** How an object stands in
  the space it is in - its cell, or the free area it is in - is said per
  axis: Left, Center, Right, Stretch; Top, Middle, Bottom, Stretch. This is
  WinForms' Anchor (and Figma's Constraints); the UI does not use the word
  or the four-armed cross, so nobody needs to know either.
- **It replaces Align and Fill** in a table's cells.
- **Dock** (Top, Bottom, Left, Right, Fill) exists in free areas only.

| Module | What | Depends on |
|---|---|---|
| `anchor-dock` | Horizontal/Vertical replace Align and Fill in cells, old projects converted on load; in free areas padding, Horizontal/Vertical against the area, Dock - docs/2026-11-03-anchor-dock.md | - |
| `card` | A «Card» look for a free area from the theme: Panel fill, radius, edge, padding. Not a type of its own, no title | `anchor-dock` |
| `card-clip` | A free area clips what it holds to its own shape, round corners included: a heading with its own fill, docked at the top of a round card, takes the card's corners. Designer, firmware (colour boards, PaperS3) and Android: a device change | `card` |
| `card-grid` | A table with an adjustable gap, its cells as cards | `card` |
| `card-blocks` | A block placed as a card: its name a plain text docked at the top, its parts filling the rest | `card`, `card-grid` |

Build order: `anchor-dock` → `card` → `card-clip`, `card-grid` →
`card-blocks`. Each module gets its own spec when the one before it is done.

Everything but `card-clip` is designer-only: an export dissolves free areas
and tables into placed objects and a box behind each area that has a look
(`freeBackground`, lib/object-groups.ts), as today.

**`card-clip`** (added 2026-11-03, asked by the user on seeing a label with
a fill docked at the top of a round free area, its corners standing out):
the export dissolves the area, so no device knows an object came from it,
and the colour boards' canvas clips to rectangles only (ColorScreenRenderer
setClip). It needs the area's shape carried with each object it held - its
rectangle and corner radius - and a round clip in every renderer that must
agree to the pixel: the colour boards' firmware, the PaperS3's, the Android
app, and the designer's preview and HIL reference. Its own firmware release.
Until then: padding of about a third of the corner radius keeps a heading
inside the round, or a heading without a fill of its own, as CamperUI's
are.
