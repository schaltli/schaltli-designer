# The palette: Parts and Prebuilt in one window with a search

Spec drafted 2026-11-06 from the user's idea, with a clickable preview
(https://claude.ai/artifact/Ek4DRsYz3imBFcs185eW1g). **Status: every decision
taken by the user on 2026-11-06; built 2026-11-06** (916f6ae1, dd88a54b,
b5914efc). test:quick: e2e green but one spec whose own window was too
narrow (widened); the boards' red suites each green alone. `npm run
test:all` still to run (the user, later).

## Objective

With every Home Assistant entity a broker announces on the Prebuilt tab, the
ribbon is too narrow: 54 things from the test fixtures scroll sideways
without end, and a name can only be found by looking at all of them (the
user, 2026-11-06: «Die Menge an HA Entities macht mich fertig»). The tools
of the Parts tab do not need the ribbon's height either.

After this work there is no ribbon. What goes onto a screen is in the
**palette**, a window like Visio's Shapes: two tabs, «Parts» and «Prebuilt»,
a search field, and below it the entries as rows in groups that open and
close. It floats over the canvas, is docked on the left, or is minimized
to a rail of two icons.

Success looks like: with all 54 fixtures announced, typing «licht» shows the
lights and nothing else; a click on «Leselicht» and a click on the screen
place its card, as the tile did.

## Decisions (the user, 2026-11-06)

1. **Floating, docked or minimized.** The palette floats over the canvas,
   moved by its title bar and resized at its edges; a button in the title
   bar docks it as a column on the left, beside Screens, and floats it
   again. A second button minimizes it to a rail at the canvas's left edge,
   about 40 px wide, with the two tabs' icons one under the other: a click
   on one opens the palette on that tab, floating or docked as it was. It
   never closes altogether, so it is always found in the same place.
2. **Two tabs:** «Parts» and «Prebuilt», with the icons the ribbon tabs have
   today (one toy brick, two on each other).
3. **One search field** under the tabs. What is typed stays when the other
   tab is chosen. It filters the open tab by the entry's name and its
   group's name; matching groups open by themselves while something is
   typed. Nothing matching: «Nothing matches «…».».
4. **A row per entry:** its icon and its name. A prebuilt's parts and an
   entry that cannot be placed are told in its tooltip, as on the tiles
   today («Not supported: the value template: …», greyed).
5. **Prebuilt grouped by device**, alphabetically, each group opening and
   closing, its count beside its name. **Parts grouped** as the ribbon is:
   Show, Operate, Draw, Arrange, Tables.
6. **No ribbon.** The tools leave it, and with them the ribbon itself. No
   button in the top bar: the palette, or its rail, is always there.
7. **No Select tool.** Placing ends in selecting, as Esc and a second click
   on the entry in hand do (docs/2026-11-05-quick-blocks.md, decision 7).
8. **The first time,** the first group of each tab is open and the others
   closed: Show on Parts, the first device on Prebuilt.
9. **Strictly alphabetical:** no device first, VanPi neither.
10. **Ctrl+K** opens a minimized palette and puts the focus in its search
    field; open already, it only puts the focus there.
11. **No Row tool.** It placed an icon, a label and a switch as a row of a
   table; since a prebuilt of one part is a card (2026-11-06) it was the
   only way to such a list, and nobody looked for it. A list by hand is
   Text and Switch set one by one and snapped with Alt. Tables, snapping
   and the rows standing in projects stay.

## Behaviour

- **Placing** as the tiles do today: a row clicked puts its entry in hand -
  the row shown pressed - and it hangs at the pointer once over the screen;
  pressed and dragged it is carried at once; let go over the palette,
  nothing is placed. The carry, Alt, Ctrl, Esc and the hint at the pointer
  are unchanged (docs/2026-11-03-placing.md, docs/2026-11-05-quick-blocks.md).
- **Prebuilt's footer:** how many things from how many devices, and
  **Refresh** (as the tab has it today). «Listen to Broker» pointing to the
  devices that announced themselves opens the palette on Prebuilt.
- **Parts** offered as the ribbon offers them: a type the loaded device does
  not render is not shown (decision 11 of docs/2026-09-20-control-split.md);
  Chart and Navigator as today.
- **Remembered** per browser (localStorage, wrapped in try/catch as the
  ribbon's tab is): minimized or not, floating or docked, its place and size
  when floating, the tab, the groups open. Not the search text.
- **Kept on screen:** a floating palette is never placed outside the
  window; after the window shrinks it moves back in.
- **Keyboard:** the search field takes the focus when the palette opens
  (from its rail or by Ctrl+K);
  Esc in it clears the text, a second Esc gives the entry in hand up.
  Delete, Ctrl+Z and the like typed in the field act on the field, not on
  the screen.

## Not in scope

- Searching both tabs at once.
- Sorting Prebuilt by kind (lights, switches, sensors), or a switch for it.
- Favourites, recently used entries, or dragging entries to reorder.
- A second palette, or several docking places (right, bottom).
- Changes to what a prebuilt builds or how a part is placed.
