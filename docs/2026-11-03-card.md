# Card

Spec for module `card` of docs/2026-11-03-cards.md, drafted 2026-11-03.
**Status: approved by the user on 2026-11-03; built 2026-11-03 (tasks/card-todo.md).**

## Objective

A card as CamperUI draws one - a panel a shade off the screen, an edge,
round corners, padding - can be built since `anchor-dock` as a free area
with a fill, an edge, a radius and padding, set by hand on every card. A
screen of eight cards is eight times the same four settings, and changing
the radius later is eight more edits.

After this module a card is one click: the **Card** tool places a free area
whose look comes from one place in the project, **Settings › Cards**, its
colours from the theme. Change the radius there and every card follows.

Who it is for: anyone building a screen of grouped values - the van's
battery, water, temperatures - as in docs/2026-11-03-cards.md.

Success looks like: the Card tool draws a card in Panel with an Outline
edge, round corners and padding; a heading docked at its top and a table
filling it look like the anchor-dock card without setting anything; a
radius changed in Settings › Cards changes every card on every screen; on a
device a card switches with light and dark like the rest of the theme.

**Decisions** (the user, 2026-11-03):

- Radius, edge and padding come from a **project setting**, Settings ›
  Cards, the same whatever the theme. The colours come from the theme:
  fill Panel, edge Outline.
- A **Card tool** beside Free in Tables; a free area can also be made a
  card, and back, in its properties.
- An **accent variant**, as CamperUI's battery card: the edge in Accent.

## Behaviour

- **The Card tool** (Tables, beside Free) draws a free area with the look
  Card, at the size drawn - or, clicked, at a default of 40 × 30 mm. It
  behaves as a free area in everything else: snapped into a cell, put into a
  switcher's panel, Position and Dock for what is in it.
- **Look**, a new field in the free area's «Free» section: **None**, **Card**,
  **Card, accent**. None is today's free area. The Card tool sets Card.
- **While a look is set**, the area's Fill, Stroke, Stroke width, Corner
  radius and Padding come from the look and are not shown; a note in their
  place says where they come from (built so: the panel's fields have no
  greyed state). Set back to None, the area keeps the values
  it had last, as its own, editable again.
  - Card: fill Panel, edge Outline at the card edge width.
  - Card, accent: fill Panel, edge Accent at the accent edge width.
- **Settings › Cards**, a new tab:
  - Corner radius, mm (default 2)
  - Edge, px (default 1)
  - Accent edge, px (default 2)
  - Padding, mm (default 2)
  - A small preview of a card and an accent card in the project's theme.
- Changing a value there changes every card on every screen at once, in one
  undo step.
- **On the device** a card is what a free area with a look is today: a box
  behind its objects, its colours as roles, so light and dark switch on the
  device as for every object. No device change.
- **1-bit devices** (the PaperS3): Panel resolves to white, so a card is a
  white area with its edge - as a box in Panel is today.

## Not in scope

- Clipping what a card holds to its round corners (`card-clip`).
- A title of its own: a heading is an object docked at the top.
- Shadows, gradients, a fill other than Panel.
- Cards in the table gap / grids of cards (`card-grid`), blocks as cards
  (`card-blocks`).

## Data model

```ts
// on a free area
properties.look?: "card" | "card-accent"

// project settings, every value optional with the defaults above
settings.cards?: {
  radiusMm?: number
  edgePx?: number
  accentEdgePx?: number
  paddingMm?: number
}
```

The layout pass (`layoutProject`, lib/layout.ts) writes a look's values into
the area's own properties - `fillColor`, `strokeColor`, `strokeWidth`,
`cornerRadius` (px, from mm at the project's scale), `padding` - before it
docks. Everything after it - the editor, the preview, the export, the HIL
reference - reads the area as today and needs no change. A pass that finds
the values already there changes nothing (keepUnchanged).

## Boundaries

- **Always:** designer-only; a free area without a look exactly as today;
  `npm run test:quick` per change; handbook in the same piece of work.
- **Ask first:** a card value in the theme after all; a type of its own for
  cards; any device change.
- **Never:** a hex colour on a card (roles only, or dark mode breaks on the
  device).

## Success criteria

## Decided (the user, 2026-11-03)

1. **Defaults:** radius 2 mm, edge 1 px, accent edge 2 px, padding 2 mm.
2. **A card in a cell** keeps its look and stretches as a free area does;
   `card-grid` builds on it.
