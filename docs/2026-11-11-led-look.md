# LED look

Spec drafted 2026-11-11 from the starter's block review with the user
(`tasks/bausteine-review.md`, drafts linked there). One language for
"chosen" and "on" across the switch types and the navigator: a lit
window or strip on a calm tile. **Status: approved by the user on 2026-11-11.**
Builds on docs/2026-11-06-toggle-button.md (replaces its decisions 2 and
6), docs/2026-09-20-switch-look.md, docs/2026-10-08-navigator.md and
docs/2026-11-03-card-blocks.md.

## Objective

The starter, built "the Schaltli way", looked partly ugly to the user
after the first cut of the film (2026-11-11): the Toggle Button fills the
whole tile while on and reads as a push button that triggers an action;
the Button Group «Aus | An» lights «Aus» in the accent; the navigator's
filled accent box pulls the eye away from the screen. In the review the
user chose, block by block, a language taken from cockpit keys (Korry)
and LED strips: the tile stays quiet, only a window or a strip lights.

Users: whoever builds a panel in the designer - the starter project and
its film first -, and whoever looks at it in the van.

## Decisions (the user, 2026-11-11)

### Toggle Button

1. **Three styles replace «Filled» and «Tonal»**: **Korry**, **LED
   Horizontal**, **LED Vertical** (`switchStyle`: `korry`, `led-h`,
   `led-v`). Every style is a calm tile - the card colour, its 1-px edge,
   the corner radius of a card - with the name on it; only the light
   changes with the state.
   - **Korry**: under the name a **square-cornered window** across the
     tile. On: filled flat in the LED colour, the state's label on it in
     capitals, black or white by the tone rule the designer already has
     for ink on a colour (`onColorFor`). Off: the window in the track
     colour, the label in the dim ink.
   - **LED Horizontal**: under the name a strip across the tile, 6 px
     (4" scale), rounded; lit in the LED colour while on, the track colour
     while off. The state's label stays beside the name.
   - **LED Vertical**: the same strip standing at the tile's left edge,
     the tile's full height inside its padding, the name and the state's
     label to its right (the user, 2026-11-11).
2. **Compact**: padding 8 x 10 px, name and light close together, no
   fixed height; on the 4" Korry window 22 px with 13 px capitals, name
   22 px; a smaller text size scales them as the rest of the type ramp
   does (`TEXT_STYLES`). The minimum height rule of the toggle spec
   (Checkpoint A) stays, counting the window or strip as a line.
3. **The LED colour is the existing colour property** (`switchColor`),
   default Accent - colour by function (warmth Warning, water Accent,
   waste water Accent 3, light Good; Alarm stays for faults).
4. **Asked, not confirmed: the light pulses at half strength** in the LED
   colour - a state, not a property. It replaces the ring (toggle spec
   decision 6). Where a device animates nothing (`screen.motion: false`,
   the PaperS3) the light stands still at the pulse's middle: half the LED
   colour mixed into the track, the label as asked.
5. **The state's icon, small, before the name** in all three styles
   (the user, 2026-11-11) - it replaces the disc of a named tile. **The
   alignment is fixed**: the name on the left, the word in a Korry window
   centred; no property.
6. **Migration**: every existing Toggle Button becomes **Korry**
   (`filled` and `tonal` alike), the demo «Camper» included. Switch and
   Button Group keep Filled and Tonal.

### Button Group

7. **A third style «LED»** (`switchStyle: "led"`) beside Filled and Tonal:
   no container of its own - no fill, no outline -, the segments side by
   side, a **1-px vertical line** in the edge colour between two, under
   each segment's label a 6-px strip. The chosen segment: strip lit in the
   LED colour (`switchColor`), label in the text colour. The others: strip
   in the track colour, label muted. Asked: the strip of the asked segment
   pulses as in 4. The frame and the name come from the card it sits in
   (decision 9), so the dividers end at the group's own edges.
8. **«LED» is the default for Button Groups a block builds**
   (`lib/bausteine.ts`, a choice part); a Button Group drawn by hand keeps
   Filled as its default.

### The rule for tiles

9. **The name stands in the tile, on top; the control below.** A Toggle
   Button carries its own name (decision 1). A Button Group or a Slider is
   framed and named by the **card** a block builds (`lib/block-card.ts`:
   a free area with look Card, its name docked at the top, the parts
   below) - no name property on either. For a Slider the card's heading
   carries the value too: name on the left, the live value on the right,
   the slider below across the card, its own number column off. A dimmer's
   value reads «Aus» at 0 - a rule in the heading's live text the block
   builder sets (the user, 2026-11-11).
   Edges line up by themselves: every tile fills its cell, nothing inside
   has a name column of its own, padding comes from the theme.

### Button

10. **A fourth button style «Text»**: no container, no outline - the label
    (and icon) in the colour alone, the state layer under a finger as
    for the others. For quiet actions inside a card («Nullen»).
11. **What is off is hidden, not dimmed** (the user, 2026-11-11): a part
    that means nothing while its device is off is shown by a Switcher only
    while on, as the block builder already does for `shown_when`. No
    «Dimmed when», no disabled look.

### Navigator

12. **A third active look «LED»** under «Background», beside Card and
   Accent: every icon on the card colour, the active one in the text
   colour with a 4-px strip under it in the accent, the others muted.
13. **The theme switch is an entry like the others** - a moon while dark,
    a sun while light, the same size as a screen's icon - behind a 1-px
    divider in the edge colour, instead of the pill switch. It stays put
    while the bar scrolls (navigator spec), as today.

## What it is, drawn

- `lib/toggle-button-shape.ts`: `toggleLooks` grows the three styles. A
  look gains `light: { kind: "window" | "strip"; x; y; w; h; r; fill;
  label?: { text; ink; baseline; fontId } }` per face, and an `asked`
  fill. The disc of a named tile goes - the light takes its place.
- `lib/switch-shape.ts`: the segmented layout gains the LED form -
  dividers, strips, colours per segment - beside `filled` and `tonal`.
- `lib/navigator.ts` / `navigator-entries.ts`: the active look «LED» and
  the theme entry with its divider.
- `lib/bausteine.ts`, `lib/block-card.ts`: Toggle Buttons in Korry for a
  switch part, Button Groups in LED for a choice part, a slider's value in
  its card's heading.
- Renderers draw from these only (`render-toggle-button.ts`,
  `render-switch.ts`, the navigator's).

## Export and devices

The rule of the toggle spec holds: **devices draw exported looks, they
compute nothing.** The export adds the light (rectangle, radius, fill per
face and for asked, the window's label with its ink), the group's
dividers and strips, the navigator's strip and theme entry. A device
draws filled rectangles and text it can already draw; new is the pulse -
a timer that alternates the light between its fill and the asked fill,
redrawing only the light's rectangle, as the energy diagram's dots do
(`screen.motion`).

- Firmware (4", 4.3B, Knob, PaperS3): `ProjectLoader` reads the new
  fields, `ColorScreenRenderer` draws them; the PaperS3 the still form.
- Android: the same in `ToggleButtonView`, the switch view and the
  navigator.
- No transition: older firmware is updated, not served.

## Designer

- Property panel: «Style» on a Toggle Button lists Korry, LED Horizontal,
  LED Vertical; on a Button Group Filled, Tonal, LED. «Colour» is the
  LED colour.
- Navigator: «Background» lists Card, Accent, LED; «Theme switch» stays
  (None, Near, Far) and draws the icon entry.
- Project migration on load: `filled`/`tonal` Toggle Buttons → `korry`.
- The demo «Camper» and the starter use the new styles.

## Not doing

- A glow around a lit window or strip: the drafts showed one; devices
  draw no blur, and the designer stays pixel-equal to them.
- A name property on Button Group or Slider: the card names them.
- The dimmer's «Switch + Slider» form, the frameless Toggle Button and
  column lines in tables - dropped when the dimmer became a `number`.

## Testing

- `e2e/toggle-button-shape.spec.ts` extended: the three styles on, off,
  none, asked (moving and still), pressed; plain and named; 24 bit, grey,
  1 bit; the window's ink by the tone rule.
- `e2e/switch-shape*.spec.ts`: the LED group - dividers, strips, chosen,
  asked.
- `e2e/navigator*.spec.ts`: the LED active look, the theme entry and its
  divider, scrolling with it pinned.
- Migration: a project with filled and tonal Toggle Buttons loads as
  Korry.
- Block builder: a switch part becomes a Korry Toggle Button, a choice
  part an LED Button Group, a slider's card heading carries the value.
- HIL conformance (`hil/conformance/specimens.js`): each style and state
  on every board against the designer's picture, the PaperS3's still
  asked form; Android goldens.
- `e2e/handbook-labels.spec.ts` for the new labels.

## Boundaries

- **Always:** `npm run typecheck` then `npm run test:quick` for every
  change; every live check made a permanent test; the handbook in the same
  piece of work (`handbuch/objekte/bedienen.md`, the navigator's page);
  stage single files.
- **Ask first:** anything that changes how an existing Switch or Button
  Group looks; flashing or deploying to the van.
- **Never:** geometry computed on a device; Playwright beside a running
  `test:quick`; a second `next dev` in this checkout.

## Success criteria

- The starter's Home, Wasser, Klima and MaxxFan screens look like the
  drafts the user chose, in the designer and on the 4".
- Designer, firmware on every board and the app agree to the pixel in
  conformance, nothing excused.
- Asked pulses where a device moves and stands still at half where it
  does not.
- Existing projects open with their Toggle Buttons in Korry and nothing
  else changed.
